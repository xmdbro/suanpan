The short version

Namespace. Key. Count.

URLs are the interface, so Suanpan works from a browser, a shell script, or any application that can make a request.

  1. 01

    Choose a namespace

    Use your domain, project, or any name that keeps your counters grouped together.

    your-project
  2. 02

    Name the counter

    Give the event a short key such as visits, downloads, or launches.

    visits
  3. 03

    Hit the URL

    The first hit creates a public counter. Every hit after that adds one.

    /hit/your-project/visits
